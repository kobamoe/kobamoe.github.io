The coach

Built from zero, not from a start list.

Ten years ago there was no bike in the garage and no front crawl to speak of. Getting through a 25m pool was the first project. What followed was a decade of building the sport from nothing, one training block at a time — three long-distance triathlon finishes, a personal best of sub 9 hours at Ironman Copenhagen in 2023, and a 3rd place age-group finish at Ostseeman in 2024.

By day, he's a full-time engineer, and yes, there's a PhD behind the name — but the coaching here isn't built on a title. It's built on ten years of logging every session, testing what actually works around a job and a family, and adjusting the plan when life doesn't cooperate. That's the same process your training gets.

Coaching is offered in English, German, and Japanese, based in Mönchengladbach, Germany, and delivered fully online to age-group athletes across Europe.
